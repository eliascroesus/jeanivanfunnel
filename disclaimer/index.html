The numbers, screenshots and videos on this Site (for example payout certificates, payout totals such as "$100K+ in payouts", and account screenshots) are Jean's own results, or the results of individual members of the JFX community where they are shown as community results. Payouts are not profit

Payout figures shown are gross payouts, not profit. They are before costs such as prop firm evaluation (challenge) fees, resets and new attempts, activation or monthly fees, trading platform and data fees, education and tools, and taxes. Profit is always lower than payouts, and can be zero or negative.

Prop firms and funded accounts

Many prop firm ("funded trader") accounts are simulated accounts. The firm sets the rules, decides whether you pass an evaluation, and decides whether, when and how much it pays out. Firms can change their rules, refuse or delay payouts, close accounts or stop operating. Evaluation fees are usually not refundable. We are not affiliated with, and do not endorse, any prop firm, broker or trading platform named or shown on this Site unless we clearly say so. If we ever earn a commission or other benefit when you sign up with a firm, broker or tool we mention, we will tell you.

Trading is risky

Trading forex, futures, stocks, options, CFDs, crypto and other markets involves substantial risk of loss. Leverage can work against you as well as for you, and with some accounts you can lose more than you put in. Prices can move fast and without warning. Only trade with money you can afford to lose, and never borrow money, use money you need for bills, or take on debt to trade or to buy evaluations.

Past performance, whether actual or simulated, is not indicative of future results.
