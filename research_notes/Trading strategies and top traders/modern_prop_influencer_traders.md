## 1. ICT and ICT-derived educators (ICT, TJR, TTrades, Romeo/CRT, Trader Daye/Quarterly Theory, Casper SMC, Justin Werlein): who they are, what they teach, what is verified

### Takeaway
The ICT family has the most influence on NQ/ES and forex intraday education in 2022–2026. ICT's own vocabulary has been spun off into branded sub-models: TTrades Fractal Model (HTF candle closes + CISD), Romeo's Candle Range Theory (CRT), and Daye's Quarterly Theory (time quarters/AMD). **None of these figures has a verified public track record.** ICT's own attempt to prove himself publicly in the Robbins World Cup was announced in October 2023 for the **2024** contest (not 2023). Critics, forums and fan trackers widely report that the account was blown or abandoned. ICT has never published the statements he promised, and I found no official Robbins confirmation.

#### "Romeo" (@Romeotpt, also "Raid") — Candle Range Theory (CRT)
- **Origin:** TradingView script authors credit CRT as "originally developed and shared by a trader named Romeotpt (Raid)" and credit "@Romeotpt on X". — [TradingView "Ighodalo Gold CRT"](https://kr.tradingview.com/script/UEPdjqLf-Ighodalo-Gold-CRT-Candles-are-ranges-theory); [variant script](https://in.tradingview.com/script/ZrH53vtD-Ighodalo-Gold-CRT-Candles-are-ranges-theory)

### Inferences
- **The 2023 premise does not hold up.** The public record shows ICT *announced* in October 2023 that he would compete in Robbins **2024**, with monthly and daily statements. The "Robbins Cup" controversy concerns the 2024 contest. Unless the official WCTC standings show a 2023 entry, the report should not say ICT competed in 2023.
- **The failure claims are plausible but unproven.** The repeated "blew the account" claims are consistent across critical forums, a fan tracker and commentary. Still, no source is primary: no Robbins standings entry, and no ICT statement. Report it as "widely reported, not officially confirmed". What can be stated is that ICT did not deliver the promised statements or documentary.
- **The ICT derivatives (TTFM, CRT, Quarterly Theory) re-package the same few ideas.** Liquidity sweeps ("turtle soup"), AMD/Power-of-3, higher-timeframe candle/time framing and a lower-timeframe "shift" trigger (CISD) recur in all of them. Each is monetised through mentorships and indicators, and none has a verified results record.
- **Werlein's admission is a rare case study.** By the reported account, a 200k+ follower ICT futures educator said himself that he was about break-even. This fits the base rates in Section 4.

## 2. Order-flow scalpers and competition traders: Fabio Valentini and Robbins World Cup (WCTC) champions 2020–2026

### Takeaway
Fabio Valentini (NQ order-flow/auction-market scalper) is the most prominent social-media trader with *competition* evidence. His own LinkedIn and promotional profiles cite 2nd-place finishes in Robbins quarterly scalping events (Dec 2023, Jan 2025) with returns of roughly 68–218% per period. The figures conflict across sources, and I could not check them against official standings. Recent annual WCTC futures champions are systematic or multi-asset traders, not ICT-style social-media educators:
- **2023:** Ivan Scherman, ~491%.
- **2025:** sources conflict, either Tirutrade AG at 324.7% or Paul Skarp at ~256%.

### Inferences
- **Fabio's best-supported claim is narrow:** 2nd place in a Robbins *quarterly scalping* event. That is a shorter, separate division from the annual Futures championship. His profile is the strongest among social-media futures educators because the WCTC uses real money with audited returns. Even so, the headline "#1 scalper" labels come from vendors tied to his own businesses (DeepCharts, World Class Edge).
- **Recent annual champions are not retail-influencer methods.** They are systematic/multi-asset traders (Scherman) or companies (Tirutrade AG). This contrasts with the influencer "funded trader" narrative.
- **High returns come with high variance.** Returns of 300–600% in a format ranked purely on % return reflect very high leverage. They should not be read as repeatable or as evidence for a teachable retail model.

### Gaps
- No official WCTC standings could be opened, so the following are unconfirmed: the 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2024 champions; Fabio's exact placements; Don Nadrian's results; whether Andrea Cimitan placed; and the 2025 Tirutrade/Skarp conflict.
- **Prior-knowledge leads only (unverified here):** Larry Williams (1987) and Andrea Unger (multiple titles 2008–2012) are historic champions, outside the 2018–2026 window.
- Other competitions (e.g., FTMO/Topstep trading contests, broker demo contests) were not researched.

## 3. Other social-media, SMC, supply-and-demand and forex educators: profiles, strategies, evidence

### Takeaway
Most prominent forex/SMC educators (Waqar Asim, Alex G, Mentfx, Stacey Burke, Jdub, Riley Coleman) sell courses, mentorships or Discord/Whop subscriptions, and none shows an independently verified record. Their methods fall into three groups:
- Supply-and-demand/liquidity scalping (Waqar)
- Higher-timeframe "set and forget" swing trading (Alex G, Nial Fuller)
- Wyckoff/structure (Mentfx) and session/cycle setups (Stacey Burke)

Educators with real institutional backgrounds include Anton Kreil (Goldman, Lehman, JPM, though critics dispute that he held a trading function) and Navin Prithyani (bank technical analyst, 2008). Tom Hougaard has a widely read book, but his self-published track record reportedly stops in Nov 2021.

### Cited Findings

#### Waqar Asim (@WaqarAsim10) — UK-style forex scalping, prop-firm focused
- **Strategy (third-party write-ups):**

  - Timeframes: 1H for supply/demand zones, 15m for trend confirmation, 1m for execution.

## 6. Is there evidence that social-media traders' results are real? Regulatory actions, lawsuits, fake payouts, exposés

### Takeaway
Regulators have repeatedly found trading-education earnings claims deceptive:
- **Raging Bull:** FTC settlement of $2.425M (2022) and a permanent injunction against "guru" Kyle Dennis (2023).
- **IM Mastery Academy/iMarketsLive (forex education MLM):** FTC/Nevada action alleging $1.2B in consumer losses; preliminary injunction in Aug 2025; settlements in May 2026 surrendering about $90M; CFTC fine of $150k in 2018.

Among the influencers profiled, the documented problems are community exposés and self-admissions, not regulatory findings: Casper SMC's alleged SIM account and Justin Werlein's reported break-even admission. Across all profiles, **zero** broker-verified track records were found.

### Cited Findings
- **Raging Bull (stock/options education) [OFFICIAL]:**
  - FTC settlement in March 2022: $2.425M, a ban on earnings claims without evidence they are typical, and cancellation-process fixes. The site promoted gurus who could "double or triple" accounts; testimonials claimed "$6,500 in 20 minutes" — [FTC consumer alert, Mar 2022](https://www.consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2022/03/investment-trading-operation-will-pay-millions-empty-promises); [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/antitrust/investment-site-to-pay-ftc-2-4-million-for-earnings-deception).

The AMG Capital ruling barred the FTC from seeking that money for refunds. It was part of "Operation Income Illusion" — [FTC press release, Sept 2023](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/09/ragingbullcom-stock-trading-guru-kyle-dennis-faces-permanent-injunction-result-ftc-action).
