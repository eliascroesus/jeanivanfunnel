## 1. Liquidity concepts: BSL/SSL, equal highs/lows, sweeps/raids/stop hunts, liquidity pools, inducement, IRL/ERL, draw on liquidity

### Takeaway
In ICT's framework, "liquidity" means clusters of resting stop and pending orders. Buy-side liquidity (BSL) sits above swing highs and sell-side liquidity (SSL) below swing lows. Every named ICT model starts from a **sweep (raid) of one pool**, followed by delivery toward the **opposing pool**, which is the "draw on liquidity." The sourced material this session confirms that skeleton across the models. Most fine definitions (relative equal highs and lows, IRL/ERL, HRLR/LRLR, inducement) could only be supplied as unverified background.

### Cited Findings
- **Judas swing as the archetypal sweep:** price sweeps resting stops beyond one extreme of the Asian range, then reverses in the true daily direction. On bullish days it takes sell-side liquidity below the Asian low; on bearish days it takes buy-side liquidity above the Asian high. — [LuxAlgo: Judas Swing](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/judas-swing/); [theinnercircletraders.com: Judas Swing](https://www.theinnercircletraders.com/ict-judas-swing/)
- **Market Maker Buy Model as a liquidity sequence:** "accumulate → Judas Swing down (sweep SSL) → rally to BSL target." The sell model is the mirror: "distribute → Judas Swing up (sweep BSL) → decline to SSL target." — [theinnercircletraders.com: MMBM & MMSM](https://www.theinnercircletraders.com/ict-market-maker-buy-sell-model/)
- **IPDA lookbacks as liquidity reference levels:** the highest high and lowest low of the rolling 20-, 40- and 60-trading-day windows are treated as liquidity reference levels. One vendor script also computes the midpoint. — [innercircletrader.net: ICT IPDA](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-ipda/); [FibAlgo IPDA Data Range (TradingView)](https://www.tradingview.com/script/bQrEwSDq-FibAlgo-ICT-IPDA-Data-Range/)
- **Draw on liquidity in the 2022 model:** "entry on the retrace; stop beyond the sweep; target the opposing Draw on Liquidity." — [quantum-algo: ICT 2022 Model guide](https://www.quantum-algo.com/blog/guides/ict-2022-model-complete-guide/); [algokings: ICT 2022 model](https://www.algokings.net/learn/ict-2022-model/)
- **A sweep alone is not a signal.** One educator writes that "price has to break the most recent counter-trend swing on the lower timeframe before I act." This is a community rule, not ICT's wording. — [forexmt4systems: ICT 2022 Model](https://forexmt4systems.com/education/smart-money-concepts/ict-2022-model/); [writofinance: ICT entry models](https://www.writofinance.com/ict-entry-models/)
- **CISD needs a liquidity context:** a valid CISD is said to require price to "first sweep a major high/low or tap into a higher-timeframe point of interest." — [TradingFinder: CISD](https://tradingfinder.com/education/forex/ict-cisd-trading-style/); [icttraders.net: CISD](https://icttraders.net/change-in-state-of-delivery-cisd-concept-complete-ict-guide/)
- **Unicorn model:** a liquidity sweep is required before the breaker forms, and skipping the sweep is "the most common mislabel." — [Fluxcharts: ICT Unicorn](https://www.fluxcharts.com/articles/trading-strategies/ict-strategies/ict-unicorn); [LuxAlgo: Unicorn](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/unicorn/)
- **Venom model as a stop-hunt model:** a community guide says the name comes from how price "'poisons' retail traders by triggering their stop orders, only to sharply reverse direction." — [innercircletrader.net: ICT Venom 2025](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-venom-trading-model-2025/)
- **SMT divergence:** the higher high in one correlated asset is read as "manipulation rather than genuine strength," meaning a liquidity grab not confirmed by its pair. — [LuxAlgo: SMT Divergence](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/smart-money-technique-divergence/); [TradingFinder: SMT](https://tradingfinder.com/education/forex/ict-smt-divergence/)

### Inferences
- Every ICT entry model reviewed shares one sequence: (1) identify the liquidity pool, (2) see it raided, (3) see a displacement or structure shift, (4) enter on a retrace into a PD array, (5) target the opposing pool. They are the 2022 model, Silver Bullet, Unicorn, MMXM, Judas/PO3, Venom and Turtle Soup. Liquidity mapping is therefore the precondition for all of them.
- Liquidity concepts are market-agnostic. They are applied the same way to Forex majors, NQ/ES and gold, and the only differences are which session or reference highs and lows are used.

### Gaps
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] BSL/SSL definitions:**
  - **BSL** is resting buy-stops above old highs. These come from short sellers' stop-losses and breakout traders' buy-stop entries.
  - **SSL** is the mirror below old lows.
  - Typical pools are prior day, week and month highs and lows (PDH/PDL, PWH/PWL, PMH/PML), session highs and lows (Asian, London, NY), relative equal highs and lows (REH/REL, "double tops/bottoms" that ICT calls "engineered" or "smooth" liquidity), and trendline highs and lows.
  - ICT treats equal highs and lows as bait that price is drawn to.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] Sweep/raid vs. run:**
  - A **raid, sweep or stop hunt** is a brief trade beyond the level, usually a wick, followed by a close back inside the range. This is the reversal case.
  - A **run on liquidity** trades through the level and continues.
  - The community often adds a "body close beyond = break, wick only = sweep" rule. That is a reinterpretation, not a rule ICT codified.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] IRL/ERL, as taught in the 2022 YouTube mentorship:**
  - **External range liquidity (ERL)** is the high and low of the current dealing range.
  - **Internal range liquidity (IRL)** is the FVGs and imbalances inside it.
  - Heuristic: after price takes ERL, the next draw is usually an IRL array (FVG), and after it rebalances IRL, the next draw is ERL.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] Draw on liquidity (DOL):** the pool or imbalance price is most likely heading to next, such as PDH/PDL, an unfilled HTF FVG, the NWOG, or an old high or low. In ICT's teaching, determining the DOL is synonymous with determining daily or weekly bias.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] HRLR/LRLR, from the 2022 mentorship:**
  - A **high-resistance liquidity run** means the path to the target is cluttered with opposing PD arrays and choppy swings. It is lower probability and slower.
  - A **low-resistance liquidity run** means a clean path toward stacked equal highs or lows with no opposing arrays. It is preferred.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] "Inducement"** is a Smart Money Concepts (SMC) community term, not a named ICT concept. ICT does not teach "inducement" as a separate element. He frames the same price action as short-term liquidity, meaning stops resting at the most recent short-term high or low, being taken. I found no source this session documenting ICT's own statements on the term.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] Turtle Soup:**
  - ICT borrowed the name from Linda Bradford Raschke and Laurence Connors' *Street Smarts* (1995). Their pattern faded failed new 20-day highs and lows, mocking the Turtle traders' 20-day breakout rule.
  - ICT's version: price raids an old high or low (often PDH/PDL, equal highs or lows, or the weekly high or low), fails, and reverses. Entry comes on the return inside the range or into a PD array, the stop goes beyond the raid's extreme, and the target is the opposing liquidity.
  - My turtle-soup search hit the budget limit, so none of this was verified.

## 2. Imbalance and PD-array concepts: FVG, BISI/SIBI, IFVG, BPR, CE, volume imbalance, opening gaps (NWOG/NDOG), liquidity void, order/breaker/mitigation/rejection/propulsion/vacuum blocks, PD array matrix

### Takeaway
"PD arrays" (premium/discount arrays) are ICT's catalogue of reference zones that price returns to. They come in two families: **imbalances** (FVG/BISI/SIBI, IFVG, BPR, volume imbalance, liquidity void, vacuum block, opening gaps) and **blocks** (order, breaker, mitigation, rejection, propulsion). They are used in **discount** for longs and in **premium** for shorts.
- **Consistent across sources:** the core FVG, BISI/SIBI, BPR, CE, NWOG and order-block definitions.
- **Inconsistent across community sources:** mitigation blocks, vacuum blocks, volume imbalances, NDOG timing, and the PD-array priority ranking.

### Cited Findings
**Fair Value Gap and BISI/SIBI**
- **Fair value gap (FVG):** a three-candle pattern in which the ranges (wicks) of candle 1 and candle 3 do not overlap. The gap is the space between them, an area where price "moved so fast that opposing orders could not participate." — [theicttrader.com: Balanced & Imbalanced Price Ranges](https://theicttrader.com/2024/05/05/ict-balanced-and-imbalanced-price-ranges/); [FibAlgo: ICT FVG (BISI, SIBI, CE, IFVG)](https://fibalgo.com/library/ict-fair-value-gaps); [tradingedges.org: ICT imbalance family](https://tradingedges.org/en/articles/ict-pd-arrays-explained-2026)
- **BISI and SIBI:** ICT introduced the term **BISI** for a bullish FVG and **SIBI** for a bearish FVG. — [writofinance: BISI and SIBI](https://www.writofinance.com/bisi-and-sibi-in-forex-trading/); [innercircletrader.net: ICT abbreviations A–Z](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-abbreviations/)

**Consequent encroachment (CE)**
- **Definition:** the midpoint of a gap, calculated as (gap high + gap low) ÷ 2, marked with a Fibonacci tool at 0 / 0.5 / 1. Guides describe the CE as "often more reactive than the gap edges." — [icttraders.net: NDOG](https://icttraders.net/ict-ndog-new-day-opening-gap-explained/); [fxnx: NDOG vs NWOG](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/ndog-vs-nwog-master-ict-opening-gaps-precision)
- **Use in the Silver Bullet:** the usual entry is at the FVG's 50% CE, though some guides use the gap edge. — [ictkillzone: Silver Bullet](https://www.ictkillzone.com/ict-silver-bullet); [backtrex: Silver Bullet](https://backtrex.com/en/blog/ict-silver-bullet-strategy-trading-guide)

**Inversion FVG, BPR and volume imbalance**
- **Inversion FVG (IFVG):** an FVG that price trades through, which invalidates it, after which it acts with the opposite polarity. A broken bullish FVG becomes resistance. Definitions vary across glossaries. — [tradingedges.org](https://tradingedges.org/en/articles/ict-pd-arrays-explained-2026); [FibAlgo](https://fibalgo.com/library/ict-fair-value-gaps)
- **Balanced price range (BPR):** the overlap of a bullish FVG and a bearish FVG in the same price zone, after price has traded through both imbalances. — [theicttrader.com](https://theicttrader.com/2024/05/05/ict-balanced-and-imbalanced-price-ranges/)
- **Volume imbalance (VI):** most commonly defined as a gap between consecutive candle **bodies** (candle 1's close and candle 2's open) while their **wicks still overlap**. One source instead calls it "a one-sided inefficiency," so definitions conflict. — [tradingedges.org](https://tradingedges.org/en/articles/ict-pd-arrays-explained-2026); [stocksandfuturestrading.com: ICT terminology](https://stocksandfuturestrading.com/inner-circle-trader-ict-terminology/); [ForexFactory TFlab: ICT abbreviations](https://www.forexfactory.com/thread/1347295-ict-trading-style-abbreviations-and-terminologies-tflab)

**Liquidity void and vacuum block**
- **Liquidity void:** "areas devoid of executed trades," typically a long one-directional run of candles. — [fxnx: ICT & SMC glossary](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/ict-smc-glossary-80-trading-terms-defined)
- **Vacuum block — conflicting definitions:**
  - **(a) Fast-move void:** a large zone formed when a trend move "sweeps through hundreds or thousands of price levels so rapidly that almost no two-sided trading occurred." This version treats it as a type of liquidity void spanning many candles, much larger than an FVG. — [ictkillzone: Vacuum Block](https://www.ictkillzone.com/ict-vacuum-block)
  - **(b) Volatility gap:** "a price gap created due to high volatility events." — [writofinance: ICT Vacuum Block](https://www.writofinance.com/ict-vacuum-block-in-forex/)
  - **(c) Unfilled void:** an unfilled liquidity void tied to stop hunting and thin liquidity that price may revisit to rebalance. — [arongroups: Vacuum Block](https://arongroups.co/technical-analyze/vacuum-block/)
  - **Indicator rule:** one TradingView script filters vacuum blocks by a minimum gap size expressed as a multiple of ATR(14). — [ICT Block By VIBE (TradingView)](https://www.tradingview.com/script/HBQwO2OD-ICT-Block-By-VIBE/)

**Opening gaps (NWOG and NDOG)**
- **NWOG:** the gap between Friday's close (4:59/5:00 PM ET) and the Sunday open (6:00 PM ET). ICT treats it as a continuing fair-value reference that price may revisit, consolidate around, reject from, or target. — [LuxAlgo: New Week Opening Gap](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/new-week-opening-gap/); [innercircletrader.net: NWOG](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-new-week-opening-gap-nwog/); [icttraders.net: NWOG](https://icttraders.net/ict-nwog-new-week-opening-gap-explained/)
- **NDOG (conflicting timing):** one guide defines it as the gap between the 5:00 PM ET close and the 6:00 PM ET reopen, which is the CME futures daily maintenance break. Another ties it to the NY midnight open. Sources also disagree on whether the Sunday reopen counts as an NDOG or only as the NWOG. — [innercircletrader.net: NDOG 5–6 PM](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-new-day-opening-gap-ndog/); [icttraders.net: NDOG](https://icttraders.net/ict-ndog-new-day-opening-gap-explained/); [FibAlgo Opening Gaps (TradingView)](https://www.tradingview.com/script/MzGvCTpQ-FibAlgo-ICT-Opening-Gaps/)
- **Gap hierarchy:** vendor guides rank the weekly gap over the daily one ("when the two point in opposite directions, defer to the NWOG"). These are trader claims, not evidence of predictive value. — [fxnx: NDOG vs NWOG](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/ndog-vs-nwog-master-ict-opening-gaps-precision)

**Order block, breaker block and mitigation block**
- **Order block (OB):** the **last opposing candle before an impulsive move that leaves an imbalance**. For a bullish OB that is the last down-close candle before a bullish displacement. It acts as support or resistance on the first return. — [ictkillzone: ICT Order Block](https://www.ictkillzone.com/ict-order-block); [theicttrader.com: OBs, Breakers, Mitigation Blocks](https://theicttrader.com/2024/03/24/order-blocks-breaker-blocks-and-mitigation-blocks/)
- **Breaker block:** a **failed order block that flips polarity**. It forms when the final swing **sweeps beyond the prior extreme** (takes liquidity) before reversing and breaking structure. — [innercircletrader.net: Breaker Block](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-breaker-block-trading/); [forexmt4systems: Breaker Block](https://forexmt4systems.com/guides/how-to/breaker-block-ict/); [fxnx: Breaker Blocks](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/ict-breaker-blocks-master-art-trading-failed-order-blocks)
- **Mitigation block — conflicting definitions:**
  - **(a) Failure swing (majority view):** a failed OB **without** a liquidity sweep. The swing fails to take out the prior high or low, and "the last up-closing candle or group of candles before that failed swing becomes the bearish mitigation block." It is described as lower probability than a breaker. — [LuxAlgo: Mitigation Block](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/mitigation-block/); [howtotrade: Mitigation Block](https://howtotrade.com/blog/mitigation-block-ict/); [fundingpips: Mitigation Block](https://fundingpips.com/blog/ict-mitigation-block-forex)
  - **(b) Continuation:** "an old, partially-mitigated order block that gets re-tested ... after the initial displacement leg has played out," traded in the original OB direction. — [innercircletrader.net: Mitigation Block](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-mitigation-block-explained/)
  - **The confusion is acknowledged:** an indicator's release notes call breaker vs. mitigation "a major misunderstanding in the community." — [ICT Order Block Pro (TradingView)](https://vn.tradingview.com/script/oObsuObl-ICT-Order-Block-Pro/)

**Rejection block and propulsion block**
- **Rejection block:** wick-based. One indicator requires a minimum wick as a % of candle range, and removes a block from the chart once price trades back into it. — [ICT Block By VIBE (TradingView)](https://www.tradingview.com/script/HBQwO2OD-ICT-Block-By-VIBE/)
- **Propulsion block:** a candle that trades into an **earlier order block** and then propels price away. "The new candle is now the level of support for higher prices: it is the propulsion block." It is a continuation array, used when price retraces into an existing OB, respects it and resumes. One source pairs it with a "mean threshold." — [TradingFinder: Propulsion Block](https://tradingfinder.com/education/forex/ict-propulsion-block/); [ictkillzone: Propulsion Block & Mean Threshold](https://www.ictkillzone.com/ict-propulsion-block); [Equiti: Propulsion Block](https://www.equiti.com/jo-en/news/trading-ideas/propulsion-block-ict-smc-how-to-identify-and-trade/); [innercircletrader.net: Propulsion Block](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-propulsion-block/)

**PD array matrix**
- **Definition:** the dealing range is split at its 50% **equilibrium**. Arrays above it are premium arrays, used for sells and as targets for longs. Arrays below it are discount arrays, used for buys and as targets for shorts. "Bullish PD Arrays only work in the discount zone. Bearish PD Arrays only work in the premium zone." — [LuxAlgo: PD Arrays](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/pd-arrays/); [ictkillzone: PD Array Matrix](https://www.ictkillzone.com/ict-pd-array); [innercircletrader.net: PD Array Matrix](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-pd-array-key-to-trade-execution/)
- **Name expansion:** usually "Premium and Discount Array"; one site expands it as "Premium and Discount Arrangement." — [theinnercircletraders.com: PD Array](https://www.theinnercircletraders.com/ict-pd-array/); [arongroups: PD arrays](https://arongroups.co/technical-analyze/ict-pd-arrays/)
- **Matrix order (one source):** old high/low → rejection block → order block → fair value gap → liquidity void → breaker → mitigation block → equilibrium. — [grandalgo: PD Array Matrix ranked](https://grandalgo.com/blog/ict-pd-array-matrix-explained); [chartwhisperer: PD arrays](https://chartwhisperer.ca/blog/premium-discount-zones-trading-guide)
- **Conflicting rankings in other sources:**
  - "Old High > Order Block > Fair Value Gap > Breaker > Mitigation Block > Rejection Block > Volume Imbalance"
  - "strongest (CISD levels, breaker blocks) to the weakest (rejection blocks, void areas)"
  - "depending on the session context"
  - There is **no single agreed community ranking**. — [smartmoneytrader: PD arrays](https://www.smartmoneytrader.co/blog/ict-pd-arrays-explained); [quantum-algo: PD Array glossary](https://www.quantum-algo.com/glossary/pd-array/)
- **Indicator scope:** one TradingView "PD Array Matrix" indicator claims to cover "every PD array ICT teaches" in 18 toggleable modules, including the OB (with a propulsion marker), breaker, mitigation and rejection blocks. — [ICT PD Array Matrix (TradingView)](https://it.tradingview.com/script/Gm0jYpoT-ICT-PD-Array-Matrix/)

### Inferences
- The community "matrix order" (old high/low → rejection → OB → FVG → void → breaker → mitigation) is a **spatial ordering**. It runs from the extreme of the premium zone down toward equilibrium, mirrored in discount. It is not a probability ranking. Later vendors have reinterpreted it as a "strength" ranking, which is why the rankings conflict.
- **Futures-specific context:** NDOG's 5–6 PM window corresponds to the CME Globex daily maintenance break. The NWOG and NDOG are therefore most naturally defined on index futures (NQ/ES) and gold futures (GC), which have a hard daily close and reopen. Spot Forex has a weekend gap but no daily halt.

### Gaps
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] ICT's 2016-17 PD array matrix, as commonly reproduced from the mentorship slides:**
  - **Premium, top to bottom:** Old High or Low → Rejection Block → Bearish Order Block → Fair Value Gap → Liquidity Void → Bearish Breaker → Mitigation Block.
  - **Discount, mirrored below equilibrium:** Mitigation Block → Bullish Breaker → Liquidity Void → Fair Value Gap → Bullish Order Block → Rejection Block → Old Low.
  - It is used together with the IPDA 20/40/60-day data ranges, which define which dealing range to split into premium and discount. This matches the community ordering above, but the slide itself was not retrieved.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] ICT's order-block rules:**
  - **Bullish OB:** the lowest down-close candle, or series of consecutive down-close candles, before an up-move that **breaks a prior swing high with displacement**.
  - **Entry:** on the return to the OB's open/high, or to the **mean threshold** (50% of the OB candle's body or range).
  - **Stop:** below the OB low.
  - **Validity:** the OB must sit in discount within the dealing range. A body close through the mean threshold weakens or invalidates it.
  - **Bearish OB:** mirror image.
  - **"Mean threshold"** (for OBs) and **"consequent encroachment"** (for gaps and wicks) are both 50% levels.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] ICT's breaker and mitigation rules:**
  - **Bullish breaker:**
    1. Price makes a short-term high.
    2. It then makes a lower low that sweeps sell-side liquidity.
    3. It then rallies through that short-term high (market structure shift).
    4. The up-close candle(s) at that short-term high, the bearish OB that failed, become the bullish breaker.
    5. Entry is on the retrace to it, with the stop below the swept low.
  - **Mitigation block:** the same structure, except the second low is a **higher low** (a failure swing, with no sweep).
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] Rejection block (ICT):**
  - Found at a swing high or low with long wicks.
  - **Bearish:** the zone from the highest candle **body** (open or close) up to the wick high.
  - Enter when price returns into the wick region; place the stop above the wick high.

  - "Event horizon" PD array, linked by the community to the midpoint between two NWOGs.
  - Suspension block (2024–25).
  - RTH Opening Range Gap (ORG), the gap between the prior 4:14/4:15 PM index close and the 9:30 AM RTH open, taught in 2023–24.

## 3. Market structure: MSS, break of structure, CISD, displacement, swing points, short/intermediate/long-term highs and lows

### Takeaway
ICT's structure vocabulary is the **market structure shift (MSS)**: a break of the most recent short-term swing after a liquidity raid, made **with displacement**, meaning an energetic move that leaves an FVG. Since about 2023–24 there is also the finer-grained **change in state of delivery (CISD)**. CISD is a body close back through the opening price of the run of candles that drove into the extreme. "BOS/CHoCH" are SMC-community terms layered on top of ICT's material.

### Cited Findings
- **CISD definition:** ICT's "body-close test for the moment price flips from being delivered in one direction to the other." It "only requires a body close through the opening price of the final consecutive up- or down-closing candles into the extreme." — [LuxAlgo: Change in State of Delivery](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/change-in-state-of-delivery/); [grandalgo: CISD](https://grandalgo.com/blog/what-is-change-in-state-of-delivery)
- **Bearish CISD:** price closes below the opening price of the relevant bullish candle, or consecutive series of bullish candles. — [TradingFinder: CISD](https://tradingfinder.com/education/forex/ict-cisd-trading-style/); [innercircletrader.net: CISD](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-change-in-the-state-of-delivery/)
- **CISD vs. MSS:** a CISD fires when delivery shifts, "often before a full ChoCh is confirmed." CISD is used for short-term changes and entries or exits, while the MSS identifies major structural changes. — [TradingFinder: MSS vs CISD](https://tradingfinder.com/education/forex/mss-vs-cisd/)
- **2022 model:** a liquidity sweep is followed by an **MSS with displacement** that leaves an FVG. Lower-timeframe confirmation of displacement and MSS is sought on 5m, 3m or 1m charts. — [quantum-algo: ICT 2022 Model](https://www.quantum-algo.com/blog/guides/ict-2022-model-complete-guide/); [innercircletrader.net: 2022 Mentorship Model](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/complete-ict-trading-strategy-2022/); [algokings](https://www.algokings.net/learn/ict-2022-model/)
- **Unicorn invalidation:** a full body close through the breaker invalidates the model. Some versions require a lower-timeframe CISD inside the unicorn zone. — [smartmoneytrader: Unicorn](https://www.smartmoneytrader.co/blog/ict-unicorn-model); [innercircletrader.net: Unicorn](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-unicorn-model/)
- Sources disagree on what the CISD reference is: the sweep candle, the final consecutive run, or a structural swing. — [LuxAlgo](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/change-in-state-of-delivery/); [writofinance: CISD](https://www.writofinance.com/change-in-state-of-delivery/)

### Inferences
- CISD (opening-price-based, fires earlier) and MSS (swing-based, confirms later) form a two-speed confirmation system. CISD gives earlier, tighter-stop entries at the cost of more false signals. MSS gives later, wider-stop but more structurally confirmed entries.

### Gaps
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] Swing points (2016-17 mentorship "market structure" lessons):**
  - A **swing high** is a three-candle pattern where the middle candle's high has lower highs on both sides; a swing low is the mirror.
  - A **short-term high (STH)** is any such swing.
  - An **intermediate-term high (ITH)** is an STH flanked by lower STHs on both sides.
  - A **long-term high (LTH)** is an ITH flanked by lower ITHs.
  - Lows are the mirror image. ICT reads trend by which of these are broken, and an ITL/ITH taken out is significant for bias.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] Displacement:** a strong, energetic, single-direction move of large-bodied candles that leaves at least one FVG. ICT repeatedly says that a structure break without displacement is not an MSS.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] BOS/CHoCH:** ICT does not use "break of structure" or "change of character." Those labels come from the SMC community, which grew out of ICT's material around 2019–2021.
- No source was found for the exact date ICT first used "change in state of delivery." Community sites attribute it to Huddleston without dating it. My background recollection places its prominence in the 2023–2024 lectures; this is unverified.

## 4. Premium/discount, equilibrium, dealing ranges, OTE (62–79%, 70.5%), standard-deviation projections

### Takeaway
A **dealing range** runs from a swing low to a swing high, or between IPDA lookback extremes. It is split at **50% equilibrium**: longs are taken only in discount (below 50%) and shorts only in premium. The **Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)** is the 62–79% retracement, with 70.5% as the "sweet spot." Targets use negative Fibonacci extensions or "standard deviation" multiples. ICT's "standard deviations" are simple multiples of an anchor range or leg, not statistical deviations.

### Cited Findings
- **Premium and discount:** the dealing range is split at its 50% equilibrium, with premium above and discount below. — [LuxAlgo: PD Arrays](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/pd-arrays/)
- **In the 2022 model:** "for a long, the entry should sit in the discount (lower) half of the swept range; for a short, the premium (upper) half." — [algokings: ICT 2022 model](https://www.algokings.net/learn/ict-2022-model/); [quantum-algo](https://www.quantum-algo.com/blog/guides/ict-2022-model-complete-guide/)
- **OTE zone:** the 62%–79% retracement with **70.5%** at its heart. Some sources use 61.8% instead of 62%, and one gives 70.2% as the centre. For a bullish setup, draw the Fibonacci from the swing low (100%) to the swing high (0%). — [TradingFinder: OTE](https://tradingfinder.com/education/forex/ict-optimal-trade-entry-pattern/); [strefatradingu: ICT Fibonacci levels](https://strefatradingu.pl/en/blog/ict-fibonacci-levels/); [backtrex: OTE](https://backtrex.com/en/blog/ict-optimal-trade-entry-ote-fibonacci-guide); [theinnercircletraders.com: OTE](https://www.theinnercircletraders.com/what-is-ote-in-trading/)
- **OTE timing:** one source says OTE trades should be entered between 8:30 AM and 11:00 AM NY. — [writofinance: OTE](https://www.writofinance.com/trading-with-ict-optimal-trade-entry-ote-zone/); [fxnx: OTE time-price matrix](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/ict-optimal-trade-entry-mastering-time-price-matrix)
- **OTE targets:** negative Fibonacci projections from −0.27 to −4.0, with **−0.27 and −0.62** the most commonly cited extensions. — [strefatradingu: ICT Fibonacci levels](https://strefatradingu.pl/en/blog/ict-fibonacci-levels/)
- **"Standard deviation" projections:**
  - **Method:** the height of an anchor range (CBDR, Asian range or Flout) is laddered out in whole and half multiples. "One 'standard deviation' equals the height of the anchor range." It is not a statistical calculation. — [LuxAlgo: Standard-deviation Projections](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/standard-deviation-projections/)
  - **Use:** one to four multiples frame where the next day's high or low may form around the London open. — [innercircletrader.net: CBDR](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-central-bank-dealers-range/); [Range Projections TFO (TradingView)](https://www.tradingview.com/script/5mCuGUSR-Range-Projections-TFO/)
  - **Conflicting source:** one lecture transcript calls CBDR a "mathematically derived standard deviations of price" approach. — [Studocu CBDR 1.0 lecture notes](https://www.studocu.com/en-us/document/harvard-university/focused-chnshis-230r-reading-local-documents-for-ming-qing-history-practice-course/cbdr-10-lecture-notes-understanding-central-bank-dealers-range/130466377)
- **IPDA data range as the dealing range:** the highest high and lowest low over the last 20/40/60 trading days, with their midpoint. — [backtrex: Dealing Range & IPDA Data Range](https://backtrex.com/en/blog/ict-dealing-range-ipda-data-range-guide); [FibAlgo IPDA (TradingView)](https://www.tradingview.com/script/bQrEwSDq-FibAlgo-ICT-IPDA-Data-Range/)

### Inferences
- OTE (62–79%) sits entirely inside the discount (below 50%) or premium (above 50%) half of the swing. It is therefore a refinement of the premium/discount rule: "deep discount" for longs and "deep premium" for shorts.

### Gaps
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] −2 to −2.5 and −4 SD of the manipulation leg:**
  - In the 2022 mentorship and 2023-24 lectures, ICT projects "standard deviations" of the manipulation leg (the Judas or sweep leg, or the first displacement leg).
  - Reversals are anticipated at about **−2 to −2.5 SD**, and expansion targets sit at about **−4 SD**.
  - The search found nothing confirming these levels. Only the −0.27/−0.62 extensions and the CBDR/Asian/Flout range multiples were sourced.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] The OTE pattern as taught in 2016-17:**
  1. A swing that has broken structure.
  2. A retrace into 62–79%, preferably during a killzone and into a coinciding PD array (OB or FVG).
  3. Stop beyond the 100% level, the swing origin.
  4. Targets at the −27% and −62% extensions, the "symmetrical price swing."
  - ICT also teaches drawing the Fibonacci on the Judas swing, or on the London session swing for NY continuation.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] Equilibrium usage:** a "fair valuation" midpoint that price often seeks. It is also used as a partial target and as a bias filter: above the 50% of the weekly or daily range means premium, below means discount.

### Takeaway
"Time and price" is central to ICT. Setups are only valid inside specific NY-time windows:

## 6. Narrative frameworks: Power of Three/AMD, Judas swing, MMBM/MMSM (MMXM), SMT divergence, intermarket analysis (DXY, bonds), turtle soup

### Takeaway
ICT's "narrative" layer explains why price moves as it does.
- **Power of Three (PO3, AMD):** each candle or session is accumulation, then a manipulation (Judas swing) against the true direction, then distribution in the true direction.
- **Market Maker Buy/Sell Models:** extend the same logic to a multi-leg swing template.
- **SMT divergence:** the confirmation tool, a non-confirmation between correlated markets at a liquidity raid.
- **Intermarket analysis:** DXY against the majors and gold, plus bonds, supplies the higher-timeframe context.

### Cited Findings
**Power of Three (PO3, AMD)**
- **Session mapping:** **Accumulation** is Asian session consolidation, **Manipulation** is a London-open false move that sweeps stops, and **Distribution** is the true directional move in the NY session. — [theinnercircletraders.com: Power of 3](https://www.theinnercircletraders.com/ict-power-of-3/); [arongroups: Power of 3](https://arongroups.co/technical-analyze/power-of-3/); [innercircletrader.net: Power of 3](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-power-of-3/)

**Judas swing**
- **What it is:** the manipulation leg. On bullish days it sweeps SSL below the Asian low; on bearish days it sweeps BSL above the Asian high.
- **Timing conflict:** sources disagree on when it happens. Some place it at the London open (2–5 AM), and others tie it to trading through the midnight open. — [LuxAlgo: Judas Swing](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/judas-swing/); [theinnercircletraders.com: Judas Swing](https://www.theinnercircletraders.com/ict-judas-swing/)

**MMXM (Market Maker Buy and Sell Models)**
- **Umbrella term:** MMXM covers both the buy model (MMBM) and sell model (MMSM). It is "a structural blueprint for how price moves from one PD Array to another." One source expands MMXM as "Market Maker eXchange Model"; others don't.
- **Stage taxonomies conflict:**
  - **Six stages:** Consolidation, Rally/Decline toward liquidity, Order Block zone, Displacement sweep, Delivery to PD Array, Distribution.
  - **Four phases:** Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution, Retracement.
  - **Five stages (TFLab indicator):** **original consolidation, price run, smart money reversal, accumulation/distribution, completion**.
- Sources: [theinnercircletraders.com: MMBM & MMSM](https://www.theinnercircletraders.com/ict-market-maker-buy-sell-model/); [theinnercircletraders.com: MMXM](https://www.theinnercircletraders.com/ict-trading-mmxm-macros-model/); [backtrex: MMXM](https://backtrex.com/en/blog/ict-market-maker-model-mmxm); [innercircletrader.net: MMBM](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-market-maker-buy-model/); [TradingFinder: MMSM](https://tradingfinder.com/education/forex/ict-market-maker-sell-model-2023/); [forexmt4systems: MM models](https://forexmt4systems.com/education/smart-money-concepts/ict-market-maker-models/)

**SMT divergence**
- **Correlated pairs used:**
  - **ES/NQ** for indices.
  - **EURUSD/GBPUSD** for Forex.
  - **Gold/silver** for metals.
  - **EURUSD vs DXY** as an inverse pair, where the logic flips.
- **Bearish SMT:** one instrument breaks its prior high and the other doesn't.
- **Bullish SMT:** one breaks its prior low and the other holds.
- **Inverse-pair example:** DXY makes a higher high and EURUSD fails to make a lower low = bullish SMT for EURUSD.
- **Swing matching:** only swings formed in the same time window are comparable.
- **How much weight it carries:** SMT counts for more at a key liquidity level or in a killzone, and is "a confirmation layer, not a standalone signal."
- **Variants:** some accept equal extremes as divergence. A "triple SMT" variant compares three instruments, such as NQ/ES/YM or EURUSD/GBPUSD/DXY. — [LuxAlgo: SMT](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/smart-money-technique-divergence/); [TradingFinder: SMT](https://tradingfinder.com/education/forex/ict-smt-divergence/); [quantum-algo: SMT](https://www.quantum-algo.com/blog/guides/smt-divergence-complete-guide/); [smartmoneytrader: SMT](https://www.smartmoneytrader.co/blog/ict-smt-divergence); [LiteFinance: SMT](https://www.litefinance.org/blog/for-professionals/what-is-divergence-on-forex/smt-smart-money-technique/); [fxnx: Triple SMT](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/ict-triple-smt-divergence-spot-smart-money); [FibAlgo SMT (TradingView)](https://www.tradingview.com/script/uuNkTbiP-FibAlgo-ICT-SMT-Divergence/); [theinnercircletraders.com: SMT](https://www.theinnercircletraders.com/ict-smt-divergence/)

**Venom model components**
- A community site presents the Venom model as a sequence of component models: **MMXM, CRT (Candle Range Theory) and SMT**. CRT is generally regarded as a community framework, not ICT's own. — [theinnercircletraders.com: Venom](https://www.theinnercircletraders.com/ict-venom-model/)

### Inferences
- **Market specificity of SMT:** NQ vs ES (and YM) for index futures, EURUSD vs GBPUSD (and DXY inverted) for Forex, and gold vs silver (GC vs SI, or XAUUSD vs XAGUSD) for metals. DXY is also used as an inverse confirmation for gold. SMT is most often cited for **NQ/ES**, where the two are highly correlated, so a divergence at a session high or low stands out.
- **PO3 is fractal.** The daily candle's "open → manipulation low → high → close" (for a bullish day) is the same template applied to the Asian/London/NY sequence. This is why the Judas-swing timing differs by source: it depends on which candle (daily vs. session) the source is describing.

### Gaps
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] PO3 on the candle:**
  - **Bullish daily candle:** opens, trades below the open (manipulation, ideally at midnight or the London open), forms the low, expands to the high, closes near the high. The candle reads open-low-high-close.
  - **Bearish daily candle:** open-high-low-close.
  - **Trading rule:** on a bullish day, buy below the opening price (midnight or daily open); on a bearish day, sell above it.
  - The same template is applied to weekly, 4H and 1H candles.
- **[UNVERIFIED BACKGROUND] MMBM as taught in the 2016-17 mentorship:**
  1. **Original consolidation** at a premium.
  2. A **sell-side curve** of lower highs, taking out short-term lows. Re-distribution phases occur along the way.
  3. Arrival at a **higher-timeframe discount PD array**, the "smart money reversal," often with SMT.
  4. A **buy-side curve** that mirrors the sell side, re-accumulating at the same levels.
  5. **Completion** back at the original consolidation, which is the target.
  - **Entries:** on the buy side of the curve, at re-accumulation and the first MSS after the SMR.
  - **MMSM** is the mirror image.

## 7. Named entry models: ICT 2022 Mentorship Model, Silver Bullet, Unicorn, OTE, Turtle Soup, London/NY reversal, Venom, others up to 2026

### Takeaway
All named ICT models are variants of one sequence: liquidity raid → displacement or MSS (or CISD) → retrace into a PD array (FVG, OB, breaker, OTE) inside a specific time window → target the opposing liquidity. They differ by:
- **2022 model:** any killzone, entry at the FVG.
- **Silver Bullet:** a strict one-hour window and the first FVG in it.
- **Unicorn:** breaker + FVG overlap.
- **OTE:** a Fibonacci 62–79% retrace.
- **MMXM:** a multi-leg higher-timeframe template.
- **Venom (2025):** a 9:30 index-open sweep after an 8:00–9:30 range.
- **8:30 model (2024).**

Rule details (minimum targets, CISD requirements, exact stops) vary between community sources and are not documented as ICT's canonical rules.

### Cited Findings
**ICT 2022 Mentorship Model** (from ICT's free 2022 YouTube mentorship)
- **Steps:**
  1. A liquidity sweep, typically in the London or NY killzone.
  2. An MSS with displacement on 5m, 3m or 1m.
  3. The displacement leaves an FVG.
  4. Entry on the retrace into the FVG, at its CE or at its edge.
- **Location:** longs in the discount half of the swept range.
- **Stop:** beyond the sweep wick.
- **Target:** the opposing draw on liquidity.
- **Reward filter:** one checklist requires at least 2:1 reward.
- The model is described as "one discretionary model rather than an automated system."
- I found "no independent performance data." — [algokings](https://www.algokings.net/learn/ict-2022-model/); [quantum-algo](https://www.quantum-algo.com/blog/guides/ict-2022-model-complete-guide/); [innercircletrader.net: 2022 Mentorship Model](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/complete-ict-trading-strategy-2022/); [grandalgo: ICT playbook](https://grandalgo.com/blog/ict-trading-strategy); [ICT 2022 Mentorship Model indicator (TradingView)](https://www.tradingview.com/script/fb4vnzXE-ICT-2022-Mentorship-Model/); [liquidityscan: build an ICT model](https://liquidityscan.io/blog/how-to-build-a-complete-ict-trading-model-step-by-step)

  3. Entry on the **first FVG that forms inside the window**, usually at its CE. "An identical FVG formed outside the killzone does not constitute a valid Silver Bullet."

**Unicorn model**

- **Stop:** beyond the breaker, past the candle that created it. One version cites a 1:2 risk-to-reward.
- **Invalidation:** a full body close through the breaker.
